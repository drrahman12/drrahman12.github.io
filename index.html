About me

I am a materials scientist based in Singapore with more than 10 years of experience in thin-film optical coatings, photonic devices, advanced metrology, and CMOS-compatible micro- and nanofabrication.

My work at the Singapore University of Technology and Design (SUTD) connects optical design and fabrication with device integration and technology transfer. My research spans multispectral color filters, thermoelectric photodetectors, structural-color coatings, and advanced nanoscale manufacturing.

I earned my PhD in Materials Science and Engineering from KAIST in 2017 and subsequently conducted postdoctoral research at the Korea Institute of Science and Technology (KIST). I enjoy collaborating across academia and industry, mentoring researchers, and developing practical fabrication processes.

Research

My research connects materials science, optical design, and micro- and nanofabrication. I work across modeling, process development, fabrication, characterization, and experimental validation, with an emphasis on scalable manufacturing and device integration.

1. Multispectral Optical Filters & CMOS Integration

Compact optical filter arrays for wavelength-selective imaging and sensing.

Binary lithography & reflow

Fabrication of multispectral Fabry–Pérot filter arrays using binary lithography and reflow (BLR), including at least 16 distinct filters produced using a single binary-lithography/reflow process, with a minimum feature size of 0.6 µm.

Optical cavity design

Design and simulation of resonant thin-film cavities to control spectral transmission through material selection and optical-layer thickness.

Post-CMOS integration

Development of fabrication flows for integrating multispectral filters with LiteON-fabricated photodiode chips, addressing process compatibility and supporting technology transfer.

Selected work: “Scaling Up Multispectral Color Filters with Binary Lithography and Reflow (BLR),” Nanophotonics (2024).

2. Structural Colors & Functional Optical Coatings

Thin-film architectures that generate color through optical interference and light–matter interactions.

Lithography-free coloration

Single-layer composite and multilayer coatings for structural color generation on metallic surfaces, including stainless steel, aluminum, and gold.

Resonant & nanocomposite coatings

Metal–insulator–metal cavities, lossy-dielectric Fabry–Pérot structures, and metal–dielectric nanocomposites for color tuning and expanded color gamut.

Durable & multifunctional surfaces

Robust optical coatings for decorative and industrial applications, including collaborative research on structural-color surfaces with antiviral functionality.

Selected work: Structural colors of gold, Advanced Photonics Research (2024); industrial-grade TiON-based coloration, Advanced Optical Materials (2026).

3. Thermoelectric & Plasmonic Photodetectors

Detector architectures that convert absorbed light into electrical signals through photothermoelectric processes.

Visible & mid-infrared detection

Design and fabrication of thermoelectric photodetectors using planar and nanostructured architectures.

Plasmonic absorption enhancement

Collaborative investigation of interband plasmonic nanoresonators to enhance optical absorption and thermoelectric photodetection.

Thin-film cavities & integration

Fabrication of resonant detector stacks, including metallization, lift-off, and device integration for direct photo-thermoelectric conversion.

Selected work: “Thin-Film Cavities with Direct Photo-Thermoelectric Conversion for Mid-Infrared Detection,” Advanced Optical Materials (2024).

4. Quantum-Confined Materials & Nanophotonic Devices

Integration of colloidal quantum dots and semiconductor nanoplatelets with optical micro- and nanostructures.

Microcavity exciton–polaritons

Collaborative research on tunable multiresonant exciton–polaritons in colloidal quantum wells coupled to optical microcavities.

Deterministic quantum-dot positioning

Contributions to controlled positioning of small numbers of aqueous colloidal quantum dots for integration into micro- and nanoscale structures.

Nanostructured light-emitting devices

Research interests and fabrication activities involving quantum-dot- and nanoplatelet-enabled optical devices, including LED and lasing architectures.

Selected work: Microcavity exciton–polaritons, Nano Letters (2025); deterministic quantum-dot positioning, Nanoscale (2024).

5. 3D Nanoprinting & Advanced Nanofabrication

Fabrication strategies for three-dimensional micro- and nanoarchitectures with tailored optical and materials properties.

Two-photon lithography & glass nanoprinting

Experience with two-photon lithography and collaborative research on nanoscale 3D printing of glass for nanophotonic applications.

Post-print processing & dimensional control

Contributions to pick-and-place processing for uniform shrinking of 3D-printed micro- and nanoarchitected materials.

Functional 3D optical structures

Collaborative work on selectively opaque and transparent structures printed from a single resin, and 3D-printed color-routing architectures for imaging.

Selected work: Glass nanoprinting, Science Advances (2025); uniform shrinking of 3D architectures, Nature Communications (2023).

6. Magnetic Thin Films & Functional Ferrites

Earlier research connecting materials processing, nanoscale structure, and magnetic behavior.

Epitaxial L1₀-FePt for magnetic recording

Doctoral research on growth and coalescence of epitaxial L1₀-FePt islands on MgO substrates for potential heat-assisted magnetic recording applications.

Microstructure & magnetic domains

Investigation of thin-film continuity and perpendicular magnetic domain structures using high-resolution TEM, X-ray diffraction, and complementary magnetic characterization.

Compositionally tuned spinel ferrites

MPhil research on lithium-substituted nickel–magnesium ferrites, examining the effects of composition on crystal structure and magnetic properties.

Selected work: Epitaxial L1₀-FePt growth, Journal of Applied Physics (2016); lithium-substituted ferrites, Physica B: Condensed Matter (2011).
